=== SCORE 6 Current status of Area A01 (Mine), 7 models: about 65 % ready for trainees. It is not ready to release yet.

Done and correct:
- Earlier per-model reviews of the haul truck, excavator, dozer and sizing station have been worked into the source files (_eq_m2p_mine.py, content/m2p_mine.py).
- Pass matching is 5–6 passes at about 15 t.
- Fleets are 9 + 2 trucks, 3 excavators and 3 + 1 dozers.
- The canopy-over-cab rule, TKPH and 300 m tyre exclusion, the sizer throughput split, sizer rotation and breaker bar, and the interlock cascades are all in place.
- Haul distance (2–8 km), grades (8 % sustained / 10 % short ramps), road width (≥ 3.5 × truck width), ROM tonnage (6.2–6.4 Mt/yr wet) and rehab rate (60–110 ha/yr) agree across the lessons and with the design basis.
- The fleet arithmetic checks out: about 27 min per cycle, about 1 Mt/yr per truck, and about 4,500 sizer hours.

Blocking items:
1. The built outputs are stale. The overview GLB embeds the pre-fix truck, excavator, dozer and station; the review renders and the station meta JSON predate the fixes. Rebuild with the overview last.
2. Overview safety and realism:
   - Trucks run head-on in a single lane on an unbermed 12 m ramp.
   - Trucks climb a 75 % slope to the tipping deck because the station ramp is cut short and overlaps the ROM pad.
   - Trucks move at about 108 km/h.
   - Caprock and ore are dug as one face, which works against the selective-mining lesson.
   - The drill and loader are not placed in the overview.
3. The drill lesson has no rules about misfires or butts, charged patterns or lightning (critical). Its blast pattern (4 × 4 m) does not match the basis (3.0 × 3.5 m).
4. Wheel loader: the bucket never tips at the dump, its lift is too low to load the truck, the 20 t per pass figure conflicts with 5–6 passes, its safety section is thin, and fleet counts do not match the tags.
5. The wheel stops need to be sized for the loader's larger tyres. Trucks are taught to edge-dump at the ROM pad and waste dumps.
6. The area-level safety content is one line.

Missing items that deserve their own models, in priority order:
1. ROM pad and blending
2. Grade-control RC rig and assays
3. Blasting-cycle lesson with an MMU
4. Water cart and grader
5. Dewatering pump and sediment pond

The design basis also needs updating to match the corrected lessons. Confidence is high on the geometry, animation and consistency findings, which were checked against code and numbers. It is medium on class envelope values, because no web verification was possible.

## m2p_blasthole_drill critical safety | content/m2p_mine.py MODULES['m2p_blasthole_drill'].safety, operation.normal, failures; META dr_pattern
ISSUE: The drill lesson leaves out the safety rules that kill drillers. It never says not to drill into or next to old sockets (butts) or misfired holes, even though the design basis lists that hazard. It gives no separation from a charged pattern, no crest stand-off, no lightning rule for charged holes, and no whip checks on air hoses. Its only blasting line is 'Blast exclusion zones after charging; misfire procedures', with no numbers. Respirable crystalline silica is mentioned only as 'dust'.
FIX: Add to safety: (1) 'Never drill into, or within ~0.5 m of, an old socket (butt) or a hole that may hold explosive. Inspect the bench for butts before collaring; any suspected misfire is reported to the shotfirer and the area is barricaded.' (2) 'Do not drill within the site separation distance of charged holes; drilling stops once charging of the pattern starts.' (3) 'Keep the tracks at least 2–3 m (site rule) from a crest; tram at right angles to the edge.' (4) 'Lightning warning (detector or site alert) → stop charging, clear the blast area and the charged pattern to the exclusion distance.' (5) 'Whip checks on all air couplings; depressurise before breaking a connection.' (6) 'Respirable crystalline silica: collar hood and dust collector working, water injection, pressurised cab, P2 respirator outside the cab.' Add F('Drilling into a misfire/butt', ['Explosive residue or a socket at the collar'], ['Butts not marked after the last blast'], ['Stop, do not withdraw the rods, evacuate, call the shotfirer; post-blast inspection marks all butts'], 'drill_string', 'safety'). Give numbers in safety: 'Exclusion typically ≥ 300–500 m for people and ≥ 100–200 m for equipment (machines facing away), as the site blast plan sets.'

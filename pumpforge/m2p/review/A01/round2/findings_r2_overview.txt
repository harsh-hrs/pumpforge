=== SCORE 7 Round-2 review of m2p_mine_overview. Web search was not available, so class values come from my own knowledge (medium confidence). Geometry, kinematics and timing were recomputed from the code in pure Python (a copy of _fillet, speeds, timed, truck_state, ik and the PASSK interpolation) and checked against the round2 renders.

The rework is substantial and most round-1 items are fixed:
- The pit ramp is now a 27 m box cut at 10 % that meets the plateau at z = 0. Lanes are keep-left: x = -37 for loaded trucks and x = -26 for empties, and road lanes are y 156.5 / 145.5. No truck spins in place.
- Truck speeds are realistic: 10.8 km/h loaded up the ramp, 32 to 40 km/h on the flat, 5.4 km/h reversing. The cycle runs in real time at about 7 min (T = 427 s; circuit A 421 s, circuit B 400 s).
- At the hopper the truck reverses straight for 14.75 m. Its rear tyres stop 1.02 m short of the 1.55 m wheel stop, then it plays the 30 s tip clip.
- Trucks never overlap. The closest approach between footprints is 4.4 m, where loaded and empty trucks pass on the station ramp.
- Each truck takes 6 passes of 30 s and the excavator clip is synchronised with the truck dwell.
- The ROM pad is 3 ha with five fingers of about 9 kt each at 35° (about 2.7 days of ROM). It no longer touches the station ramps.
- Backfill (2.5 m at 37°) and rehabilitation (surface at -4.25 m, 15.8° landform walls) now balance with the waste removed.
- The model now has grade control (collars, RC rig, grade-bin polygons), the drill on a pegged 3.0 x 3.5 m pattern, the loader, light vehicles, a water cart, a sump and pump, the safety, start-up and shut-down text, and area-specific narration.

Remaining problems:
1. The ore excavator's dig pose takes the bucket teeth 0.53 m below the grade-control clay floor (z -7.53). On the return swing the bucket comes within 0 to 0.1 m of the truck's tail corner. The swing is only 17.6°, while the excavator lesson teaches 45 to 70°.
2. The station-ramp windrows sit 2.25 m outside the ramp edges, so they float beside an embankment up to 10 m high. The tipping-deck outer edges have only 1.1 m handrails.
3. Light vehicle no. 0 is fully buried in the sediment-pond bank. The pond water slab floats about 4.8 m inside the banks, and the banks are 1.9:1, not the 3:1 the label claims.
4. The ROM pad is about 400 m of load-and-carry (including a 10 m climb) from the hopper. The loader lesson limits load-and-carry to 100 to 150 m.
5. The final basis m2p/basis/final_00_mining.json (written 05:05) now fixes an India East Coast deposit: 10–30 m ore on 5–8 m drill-and-blast benches, ramps no steeper than 8 %, 20–45 ha/yr. The overview still shows a 4.2 m free-dig ore flitch.
6. The built outputs are stale again. The truck and dozer GLBs (05:42) are newer than the overview GLB (05:04), and the learning JSON (05:03) predates content/m2p_mine.py (05:05).

Overall the model is close to trainee-ready once the excavator poses, the windrows and deck bunds, the light-vehicle and pond clash and the basis alignment are fixed.

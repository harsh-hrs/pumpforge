# PumpForge — Industrial Engineering Equipment Library (282 modules, interactive 3-D)

Procedurally built, engineering-proportioned 3-D models plus structured learning content for an Android app. The library has 282 modules, arranged and numbered by department (Valves & Piping, Pumps, Rotating Equipment, Static Equipment, Material Handling, Thermal & Process, Filtration & Separation, Alumina Refinery, Water & Utilities, Mechanical Components, Safety & Environmental, Instrumentation & Controls):

1. **23 valves & piping items** (modules 1–23, equipment numbers EQ-118 to EQ-140, phase 4): special, automated, control and safety valves; steam trap; flame arrestor; rupture disc; sight glass and flow indicator; pipe spool and manifold; orifice plate, flow nozzle and venturi tube; pig launcher and receiver; insulation and heat-tracing systems.
2. **39 pumps** (modules 24–62), classified by working mechanism following the Hydraulic Institute's rotodynamic / positive-displacement families, on the three-phase roadmap: core, intermediate, and specialist & applications.
3. **30 rotating equipment items** (modules 63–92, EQ-141 to EQ-170, phase 5): reciprocating, screw and vane compressors; Roots and side-channel blowers; axial, centrifugal, ID, FD and PA fans; steam, gas, hydraulic and expander turbines; rotary, liquid-ring and dry-screw vacuum pumps and the steam ejector; engine-driven generator, alternator, diesel genset; VFD and soft starter; magnetic coupling, torque limiter, speed reducer, belt, chain and flywheel drives; rotor assembly.
4. **25 static equipment items** (modules 93–117, EQ-171 to EQ-195, phase 6): double-pipe, air-cooled and spiral heat exchangers; jacketed vessel; agitated, pressure, autoclave, packed-bed and fluidized-bed reactors; deaerator, feedwater tank, hotwell, surge vessel; knockout drum, two- and three-phase separators; scrubber and demister vessels; chimney, silencer, gas holder; LPG bullet, cryogenic (LNG) tank, floating-roof tank and pressure sphere.
5. **32 material handling & heavy machinery items** (modules 118–149, EQ-196 to EQ-227, phase 7): apron, vibrating, grizzly, rotary airlock, screw and weigh feeders; vibrating and trommel screens; jaw, cone, gyratory, impact (HSI), hammer and roller crushers; stacker, reclaimer, stacker-reclaimer and bucket wheel excavator; tripper, shuttle, drag-chain and vibrating conveyors; pneumatic conveying; screw jack; gantry, jib and monorail cranes/hoists; electric winch; hydraulic power unit, cylinder, accumulator and control valve assembly.
6. **25 thermal & process items** (modules 150–174, EQ-228 to EQ-252, phase 8): kettle reboiler, preheater, economizer, superheater, rotary air preheater, packaged steam generator, HRSG; flash, falling-film, forced-circulation and multiple-effect evaporators; spray, fluid-bed, flash, paddle and vacuum dryers; fluidised bed combustor, thermal oxidizer, rotary kiln incinerator, gas-fired heater, electric arc furnace; run-around heat recovery, quench tower, cooling coil and cooling-water system.
7. **18 filtration & separation items** (modules 175–192, EQ-253 to EQ-270, phase 9): cartridge and plate-and-frame membrane filters; MF, UF and NF systems; disc-stack, decanter and basket centrifuges; wet drum magnetic separator; flotation cell and DAF; CPI oil–water separator and coalescer; molecular sieve and activated alumina adsorbers; desiccant air dryer; TEG gas dehydration; sand trap.
8. **25 alumina refinery (Bayer process) items** (modules 193–217, EQ-271 to EQ-295, phase 10): bauxite stockpile reclaimer, washing drum, grinding mill, slurry mixing tank; digestion autoclave and heater; multi-stage flash vessel; desilication tank; precipitation tank agitator; seed classification, classification hydrocyclone; red mud washer, mud settling tank, sand separator; hydrate filter and washing; fluid-bed and alumina coolers; storage silo and pneumatic conveying; causticization tank; oxalate removal and sodium oxalate filter; liquor clarifier; dust collection.
9. **25 water treatment & utilities items** (modules 218–242, EQ-296 to EQ-320, phase 11): clariflocculator, flash mixer, flocculation tank, lamella clarifier; chlorination, UV and ozonation disinfection; demineralization plant, mixed-bed polisher, softener, degasser tower; chemical preparation tank and injection skid; instrument air dryer, air receiver & refrigerated dryer package; PSA nitrogen generator and liquid-nitrogen storage; steam distribution header and condensate recovery; cooling tower basin, fan and gearbox; emergency diesel firewater pump; sewage lift station; effluent treatment plant.
10. **25 mechanical components & maintenance items** (modules 243–267, EQ-321 to EQ-345, phase 12): labyrinth seal, oil seal, gland packing, mechanical cartridge seal; tilting-pad thrust bearing, journal bearing, bearing housing; expansion (gear) coupling, universal joint, torsional coupling; shaft sleeve, wear ring, impeller; motor rotor and stator; gear assembly; lube oil skid, grease, centralized and oil-mist lubrication; dial-indicator and laser shaft alignment, dynamic balancing machine; vibration and online condition monitoring.
11. **15 safety & environmental items** (modules 268–282, EQ-346 to EQ-360, phase 13): emergency pressure relief system, blowdown tank; closed and open drain systems; flare stack and flare knockout drum; gas scrubbing system, venturi wet scrubber, dry scrubber, baghouse dust collector; ventilation system, emergency ventilation fan; fire & gas detection, firewater monitor, foam firefighting system.
12. **Instrumentation & Controls** — reserved department (future expansion).

Every module has the same **13 sections**:

| # | Section | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction (definition, applications, key points) | `output/learning/<id>.json` |
| 2 | 3-D model, rotatable and zoomable | `output/glb/<id>.glb` |
| 3 | Exploded view | `Explode` clip / `explode_offset` per part |
| 4 | Cross-section | split meshes + `section` material (cutaway) |
| 5 | Working principle, animated | `Operate` clip + step list with part highlights |
| 6 | Flow animation | `<id>_flow.glb` overlay (tracers for jet/airlift) |
| 7 | Components, with the function and material of every part | `output/meta/<id>.json#components` |
| 8 | Engineering parameters: design-class ranges plus an empty `reference_model` datasheet slot | learning JSON |
| 9 | Installation (foundation, alignment, piping, commissioning) | learning JSON |
| 10 | Maintenance (routine, periodic, overhaul, lubrication) | learning JSON |
| 11 | Failure analysis (symptoms, causes, remedies, affected part) | learning JSON |
| 12 | Video tutorials: three in-app tours (intro, operation, maintenance), each a narrated script that drives the camera, cutaway, explode, animation and overlays | learning JSON |
| 13 | Assessment: quizzes, including "identify the component" questions tied to 3-D parts | learning JSON |

**Flagship modules** have extra teaching overlays, which are separate GLBs that the app can toggle:

- **Vertical Turbine Pump.** A WPIL WVT-series (WVT-30-18) learning case. Overlays show stage-by-stage pressure, the down-thrust path to the motor thrust bearing, and sump levels with submergence (ANSI/HI 9.8).
- **Slurry Pump.** An x-ray map of wear zones by severity: throatbush, impeller eye, cutwater, liner and back shroud.
- **Horizontal Multistage Pump.** Overlays show stage-by-stage pressure build-up, and impeller thrust against the balance-drum force, with the leak-off line.

Engineering parameters are **typical published ranges for the design class**, marked "to be verified". They are not ratings of a specific product. Each module has an empty `reference_model` block to fill in from the chosen manufacturer's datasheet.

## Learning player (v1.1)
`viewer/library.html` is the full web player and the reference for the native Android app. On top of orbit / tap-to-inspect / explode / cutaway / operate it adds:

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **X-ray** | Ghosts every static part so the moving internals (impellers, pistons, spools, discs, pigs) stay visible while the animation runs |
| **Live flow** | Animated particles run along each flow path, coloured by fluid (liquid, steam, gas, condensate, oil). Gated paths (three/four-way valves, manifold branches) only flow while that port is open in the animation |
| **How it works** | Step-by-step player: each working-principle step moves the camera, highlights the parts and shows the right overlay; manual or auto (6 s per step) |
| **Library** | Search by name, EQ number, category, subcategory or component; bookmarks (★); progress per module (opened, best quiz score) |
| **Tools tab** (for engineers / SMEs) | Calculators: pump power, affinity laws, NPSHa, specific speed, valve Kv/Cv, differential-pressure flowmeter (ISO 5167, simplified), pipe friction (Darcy / Swamee-Jain), insulated-pipe heat loss, unit converter. Editable reference datasheet (saved on the device, copy as JSON), notes per module, side-by-side compare with any other module, distance measurement on the model, isolate / hide parts |

Progress, bookmarks, notes and datasheets are stored on the device only.

## Android app (APK) — fully native since v2.0
`python3 native/tools/build_apk.py --version-name 2.1.0 --version-code 13` builds `native/build/PumpForge-2.1.0-native.apk` (≈ 22.6 MB).
The app is **fully native**: C++17 + OpenGL ES 3.0 on `android.app.NativeActivity`, with no WebView, no JavaScript and no Java code.
It contains all 282 modules in a compact native model format (16-bit positions, 8-bit normals; 18 MB instead of 56 MB),
every lesson section, quiz, tour (with text-to-speech) and engineering tool of the web library. Phones use a stacked
layout and tablets a side-by-side layout, with light and dark themes. v2.1 redesigns the interface: Home, Catalog,
3D Viewer, Bookmarks and More screens with bottom navigation, live 3-D thumbnails, Learning Paths, Compare and Settings. See [native/README.md](native/README.md).
An Android Studio (Gradle + CMake/NDK) project for the same sources is in [native/android-studio](native/android-studio/README.md).
The earlier WebView app (v1.x) is still buildable with `python3 android/build_apk.py` ([android/README.md](android/README.md)).

## Build
Requirements: Python 3.11+ with `bpy` (Blender as a Python module, tested with 5.2 LTS).
```bash
pip install bpy            # ~400 MB
python3 build.py           # all models
python3 build.py aodd_pump # one model
python3 build_content.py   # learning modules (no Blender needed)
python3 tools/check_library.py   # recheck all 282 modules
python3 tools/update_docs.py     # refresh README catalogue
```
Building is deterministic: the GLB and JSON files are regenerated from source in seconds.

## Accuracy statement
These are explanatory engineering models based on typical published arrangements, such as HI and API nomenclature and the standard involute gear form. They are not manufacturer drawings. Standards editions are marked "to be verified" where they have not been confirmed against the current publication.
